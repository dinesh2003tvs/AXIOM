# AXIOM Role-Based Prototype

## Stakeholder views
- CXO / Leadership: executive portfolio intelligence, leadership attention, health/lifecycle charts, read-oriented access.
- PMO: governance, allocation, exceptions, portfolio control and management.
- PM: delivery workspace, assigned projects, milestones, actions and execution.

The page structure remains consistent across roles; content, KPIs, actions and emphasis change by role.

## Lifecycle
Open -> Ready for review -> In Progress -> Closed

## Prototype boundary
Business records and UI preferences persist locally in the browser, with uploaded file contents in IndexedDB. Live identity, databases, Teams/Outlook, AI services and enterprise integrations are not connected. Role selection is a prototype view switch, not security enforcement.
